# Entra ID Overview

This section is for the practical side of working in Microsoft Entra ID.

The focus here is not abstract theory alone. It is the operational knowledge that becomes useful again and again:

- how identity pieces fit together
- what to check when something breaks
- how to explain decisions clearly
- how to turn repeated problems into reusable reference notes

## Planned areas

- architecture and mental models
- tenant and access operations
- app integrations and identity flows
- troubleshooting and incident notes
